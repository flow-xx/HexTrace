# Privacy Policy — HexTrace and HexTrace: Words

*Last updated 31 August 2026. Applies to **HexTrace** (`com.alistaireaves.hextrace`) and
**HexTrace: Words** (`com.alistaireaves.hextracewords`) for iOS.*

## The short version

**These apps collect nothing.** No account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash
reporting, no third-party SDKs. Neither app makes a network request of any kind: every
puzzle is generated before the app is built and ships inside it, so there is nothing for
either app to fetch and nothing to send.

## What is stored, and where

Your progress — which levels you have finished, how many moves you took, and your
settings — is written to **your device's local storage** and stays there. It is not an
account, it is not backed up to any server operated by us, and it is not readable by
anyone but the app on your phone.

Deleting the app deletes it. There is no way to recover it and no copy anywhere else.

## What is not collected

To be explicit, because "we collect nothing" is easier to say than to verify:

- No name, email address, phone number, or any other contact detail.
- No device identifiers, advertising identifier, or IP address.
- No location data of any kind.
- No usage analytics, session recording, or performance telemetry.
- No purchases — neither app contains in-app purchases or subscriptions.

The apps contain **no third-party analytics, advertising, or attribution SDKs.** The only
third-party code either app contains is [Capacitor](https://capacitorjs.com), the
open-source shell that hosts the game, and one official Capacitor plugin —
**`@capacitor/app`**, which reports when the app moves between the foreground and the
background so the game can restore its sound effects on returning. It reads no data and
sends nothing, and neither does Capacitor itself.

## Children

The apps are suitable for all ages and collect no data from anyone, including children.
There are no advertisements, no external links out of the app, no user-generated content
and no communication features.

## Beta testing

While the apps are distributed through **TestFlight**, Apple collects information about
installs, sessions and crashes and shows it to us in aggregate, and TestFlight lets you
send feedback and screenshots if you choose to. That collection is Apple's, governed by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and it happens whether or
not an app does anything at all. We receive only what Apple shows every developer:
aggregate counts, crash reports, and any feedback you deliberately send.

## Third-party data used to build the game

The word list in HexTrace: Words is built from published academic and public-domain
sources — Brysbaert et al.'s concreteness norms (CC BY 4.0), wordfreq (Apache 2.0), the
Moby Hyphenation List (public domain) and WordNet. **This is data that goes INTO the game,
not data about you**; it is credited in the app under Settings → About & credits. None of
it is personal data and none of it leaves your device.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. Since the apps collect
nothing, any change would be to say so more clearly rather than to widen what is taken.
